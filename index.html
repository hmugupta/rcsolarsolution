Our Solar Solutions

Three ways to go solar

The right system depends on your grid connection, your budget and whether you need backup power. Here's how the three main options compare.

ON-GRID

Connected to the grid

Generate your own electricity and cut your monthly bill, without needing a large battery bank. Best for properties that already have a stable grid connection.

  • Residential buildings, shops, offices
  • Commercial & industrial establishments
  • Efficient use of rooftop space
OFF-GRID

Independent of the grid

Panels, inverter, battery, mounting structure and electrical protection working as one system — built for places without reliable grid electricity.

  • Farms & rural or remote areas
  • Homes needing independent power
  • Backup applications
HYBRID

Solar, battery and grid

Use solar power directly, store the excess in batteries, and fall back on the grid when needed — for the best balance of savings and reliability.

  • Homes, offices and shops needing backup
  • Better day-to-day energy management
  • Greater overall energy independence
